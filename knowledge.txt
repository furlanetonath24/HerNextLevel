# High School Math and STEM Summer Programs Knowledge Base

## Program 1: IMA-MathCEP Math Modeling Camp
- Organization: University of Minnesota
- Location: University of Minnesota (Non-residential, Twin Cities preference)
- Cost: Free
- Dates: June 22–June 26
- Application Deadline: May 31
- Target Audience: High school students who have completed Calculus.
- Description: High schoolers use mathematical models to analyze real-world situations, such as traffic flow on a bridge or the impact of climate change on natural resources. Requires a math teacher recommendation.

## Program 2: SUMaC (Stanford University Math Camp)
- Organization: Stanford University
- Location: Online and Stanford University (Residential)

- Dates: Online Session 1 (June 15–July 3), Online Session 2 (July 6–July 24), Residential Session (June 21–July 17)
- Application Deadline: February 2
- Target Audience: Academically advanced high school students.
- Description: Offers intensive online and residential experiences in advanced mathematics through lectures, guided research, and group problem-solving. Enrolls 64 virtual and 40 residential students.

## Program 33: JHU APL ASPIRE
- Organization: Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory
- Location: Laurel, MD (in-person; students must arrange own transportation)
